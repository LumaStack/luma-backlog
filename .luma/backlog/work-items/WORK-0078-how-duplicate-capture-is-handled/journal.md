## ▶ 2026-09-09

the three capture modes differ by WHICH COST the person refuses to pay — turns, git noise, or the risk of the idea being reshaped before it is saved — not by how thorough they are
read as degrees of thoroughness they collapse into one dial and the middle one becomes the safe default forever; read as three costs, none is a degraded version of another — whoever refuses to spend turns is not doing a worse capture, they are paying with something else
the framing doubles as a completeness test: a fourth mode has to name a fourth cost, or it is one of these three renamed
that sentence was written into a commit message and a PR body first and was nearly lost — neither is read by somebody opening the record, and the maintainer had to ask whether it had been captured before it reached the record at all
