## ▶ 2026-09-09

measured before capturing: advances is carried by 9 of 51 tasks in this corpus, all nine on WORK-0001. the other 42 carry nothing, and task new has no --advances flag, so the field is recommended and unaskable
so the condition would fire on 82% of tasks here, almost all legitimate — which reorders the work. warn-versus-refuse is premature; the first question is whether the data can be collected at all, and when-a-work-item-splits already says the question belongs at creation
the scope-creep warning probably should not fire on the TASK: one task advancing nothing is ordinary, and the fourth outcome written to accommodate one is the finding. the pattern is the signal, not the instance
and absence is legal by design — spec 4.5 makes advances recommended and says 'not every outcome needs a task', so treating it as an error means changing the specification rather than adding a check
