the rule inverts the current procedure's default — backlog-capture's quick mode says 'do not check for duplicates' and 'do not offer opinions, not even good ones', where this says recording them is the default and cost is the only brake
so quick capture stops being a different KIND of capture and becomes the same capture with a budget. that is a smaller change to specify and a harder one to implement, because 'would this take more than a turn' has to be judged before the work is done
related recorded on the record itself: WORK-0029 separate quick capture from thoughtful capture, possibly a duplicate and already unprepared where this is captured; WORK-0052 quick capture has nowhere to put a long thought, the narrow case of the same tension
added: define the sections that get captured and a template to fill in, with one section being 'In the capturing agent's own words' for the agent's interpretation
that section is not the same as the analysis agents write today — analysis is commentary about relations and prior art; interpretation is the agent saying what it UNDERSTOOD the person to mean, which surfaces a misread in the same turn rather than three sessions later
the separator the template needs already exists as an unwritten habit — 'everything above is the maintainer's, everything below was added by the agent' appears across this corpus and is documented nowhere. the template is where it stops being convention and becomes a rule
everything the agent had is now ON the record rather than in the conversation — nine concerns, plus an interpretation section written to demonstrate the template section this record proposes
the three quiet violations are the useful finding: TITLE, DESCRIPTION and KIND are all written by the agent today, all interpretation, and the can/cannot lists do not reach any of them. the title is the most-read part of a record and is never the person's words
possible resolution to the cost problem — bound the EFFORT rather than predict the OUTCOME: one listing pass, plausible records opened, stop, and record what was not looked at. the procedure already asks for the second half with 'finding nothing is a result and worth one line'
and the separator may pay for itself twice: backlog-capture currently REFUSES to append raw text to a record past captured because it 'puts unreviewed text beside reviewed text with nothing marking the difference' — a guaranteed separator removes that reason, letting capture keep its promise never to refuse
added by the maintainer: how people AMEND a work item is undecided — a second pass may want its own raw entry and its own agent entry, or the material may fold into the existing ones
