# The experiment ran and left a complete record of itself

**This is the outcome about doing it, not about what it found.** An experiment
described but not run proves nothing, and one run without a record proves nothing
to anybody who was not there.

**Written after the work, which the record is honest about.**
[[work-items/WORK-0032-how-goalpost-fitting-is-discouraged-without-being-prevented]]
ranks *written after the fact, and still proven* second of three and permits it.
The third check exists because that ordering is exactly where goalposts move: an
outcome written afterwards can be fitted to what happened, and the journal
disagreeing with the transcript is the cheapest evidence that it was not.
